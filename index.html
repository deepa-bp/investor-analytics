Where these sources come from

The five sources are based on Millburn's public filings and coverage. The volumes on this page are sample data.

  • Website (direct): individuals who find Millburn through millburn.com, its Insights research and contact forms.
  • Referral (direct): introductions through existing relationships. Millburn's Form CRS says its retail clients are financial planning clients with an extensive overall relationship, and that it acts as placement agent for its funds.
  • RIA advisers: registered investment advisers placing clients. The Multi-Markets Fund 10-K for 2025 records RIA platform fees, and Series D carries a 0.75% ongoing fee.
  • Broker-dealer agents: the same 10-K says units are sold "through the General Partner and selling agents", with Series A paying selling agents 2% a year. Opalesque notes Millburn historically sold through broker-dealers such as Merrill Lynch.
  • Alt platforms: alternative-investment and separate-account platforms that advisers use to buy funds. Opalesque lists separate-account platforms as a Millburn channel; which platforms Millburn uses today is an assumption to confirm.
  • Outside this view: the Catalyst/Millburn mutual funds (Hedge Strategy MBXIX, Dynamic Commodity DCXIX) and the European UCITS fund. Their investors are held by the fund sponsor or platform, so only asset flows could be added, not individual journeys.
